Los Angeles

Melady Lab · USC

Graduate Research Assistant

I'm on three projects in the lab. These four pieces are mine.

  • Time-series RAG. I built the retrieval engine for a RAG system for time series that finds past series similar to an input series or text. This augments a forecasting model with real historical analogues to work from. It searches 33.4M observations across 16 domains and beat the previous engine head-to-head on 92% of queries.
  • Pretraining corpus. A config-driven ETL pipeline that built 500K aligned time-series and text records across 14 domains, for continued pretraining of a time-series foundation model.
  • Embedding baseline. The control for the lab's main model. A frozen TimesFM-2.5 encoder feeds Qwen3-1.7B through LoRA, to test whether a language model can read a series straight from continuous embeddings.
  • Evaluation harness. A reproducible harness that runs 8 language and time-series models across TemporalBench, TimeSeriesExam and CiK, covering 3,521 multiple-choice questions and 355 forecasting instances. One result: Llama 3.1 8B forecasts significantly worse when it can see the series (p < 0.05).

Paper in submission.

Projects02
May 2026

Adaptive RAG — biomedical domain

Retrieval

A router that decides whether a biomedical question is better answered by Llama 3.1 8B alone or by retrieving over a FAISS index of PubMed abstracts first. I trained three routers — XGBoost, DistilBERT, and a Llama LoRA adapter — on weak supervision from MedHallu, then evaluated on PubMedQA.

Retrieving every time scored highest at 78% against 53% for the model alone; the XGBoost router reached 69% while skipping retrieval on a good share of questions, which is the tradeoff the project was actually testing.
